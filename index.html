🧠 Golden rules

Short sessions work.20-45 mins per subject, with a break in between, beats a 3-hour grind every time.
Active beats passive.Flashcards, Seneca and past papers beat re-reading notes.
Set a timer.Work until it goes off. Short break. Repeat.
Take your day off.One day off a week is part of the plan - rest is revision too.
Revision Timetable
6 weeks · builds from 20 min sessions up to 45 mins per subject · one day off a week (usually Friday) · Exams 16-26 November. Revision is always the night BEFORE each exam.
